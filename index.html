Soccer Challenge data packs

This host serves the player dataset for the Soccer Challenge app, split into downloadable packs. The app reads one file to find the rest:

dataset/index.json

Pack 1 is the baseline: 200 well known players, shipped inside the app, so a first launch is playable offline. Packs 2 and up are downloaded in the background and widen the solo modes. Each pack carries its own version and SHA-256 digest, which the app verifies before it trusts a download.

Player careers come from Wikidata, released into the public domain under CC0. Club names and dates are plain text. No crests, kits or photographs are published here.
